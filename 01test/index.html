Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship means using technology and the internet safely, responsibly, respectfully, and appropriately.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the rules and laws that govern how people use technology, computers, and the internet.

What is AUP?

It's a set of rules explaining what users can and cannot do when using a company's, school's, network's, or software platform's services.

What is Plagarism?

Using someone else's words, ideas, work, or creations and presenting them as your own without giving proper credit.

It doesn't matter whether it's done on purpose or by accident-it's still plagarism.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty means cheating or being dishonest in school or college work to gain an unfair advantage.

In simple terms: it's cheating or being dishonest in school work.

What are the rules for AI generated content?

AI-generated content may be used only when allowed and only in responsible ways.
