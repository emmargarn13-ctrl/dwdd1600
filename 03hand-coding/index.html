Tools

Where to begin

Some good cooking tools to get to start out with are things such as a set of pots and pans, they don't need to be anything fancy, just something to get you started. The next thing would be to get cooking utensils, I would suggest silicon utensils or wood utensils to start out with as these are very both very durable and can last you a very long time! You will want to get several spoons, spatulas, ladels, a whisk, etc. You should also get you cutting boards and a knife set, I would suggest you get something a little better quality as these will be something you use for a lot of recipes in the future and you will want something that can get the stuff done that you need to. MasterChef is always a great place to go for good quality knives.

Why do we use these tools?

There is obviously a use for each of these items. You will want to know what each items use is in the kitchen before we begin any cooking. Familiarize yourself with these tools and their use before moving on!

Chef's Knife
Chopping, slicing, and dicing vegetables, meat, herbs, and other ingredients.
Paring Knife
Cutting and peeling small foods, such as apples, strawberries, or potatoes.
Serrated Knife
Cutting foods with a tough outside and soft inside, especially bread and tomatoes.
Cutting Board
Providing a safe, sturdy surface for cutting and preparing ingredients.
Nonstick Skillet
Cooking foods such as eggs, vegetables, pancakes, chicken, and burgers.
Saucepan
Cooking smaller amounts of food, such as sauces, rice, oatmeal, and soup.
Large Pot
Cooking larger amounts of food, especially pasta, soup, chili, and potatoes.
Baking Sheet
Baking cookies and roasting vegetables, potatoes, chicken, and other foods in the oven.
Wooden Spoon
Stirring food while it cooks and mixing ingredients together.
Spatula
Flipping and turning foods such as eggs, pancakes, burgers, and vegetables.
Tongs
Picking up, turning, and serving hot foods without touching them directly.
Whisk
Mixing ingredients together and adding air to things like eggs, sauces, and batter.
Can Opener
Opening cans of food such as beans, vegetables, soup, and tomatoes.
Vegetable Peeler
Removing the skin from vegetables and fruits such as potatoes, carrots, and apples.
Mixing Bowls
Mixing ingredients together and preparing foods before cooking.
Measuring Cups
Measuring larger amounts of ingredients, especially liquids and dry ingredients.
Measuring Spoons
Measuring small amounts of ingredients such as salt, spices, baking powder, and oil.
Colander
Draining water from pasta and rinsing fruits, vegetables, and other foods.
Food Thermometer
Checking the internal temperature of food, especially meat, to make sure it has been cooked safely.
Oven Mitts
Protecting your hands from hot pots, pans, and baking dishes.
